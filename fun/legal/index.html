The fine print

Contact

Questions, takedown requests or press: message @gregthefrogfun on X.

Privacy

  • You do not need an account to visit Freg Island.
  • When you fly around, your fly's position, name and colour are shared live with other visitors. They are not stored.
  • We store notes and questions you leave for Greg, plus a random visitor id kept in your browser and a one-way hash of your IP address, only to stop spam and limit how often notes can be sent.
  • If you choose to pay optional rent in SOL, the public transaction signature, amount and your fly's name are stored so Greg can thank you. Blockchain transactions are public by nature.
  • Your browser keeps small settings (sound, quality, fly name). Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • We use Supabase to store data and Google Fonts for typefaces. We do not sell data and do not run ad trackers.

Terms

  • Greg is a fictional cartoon character run by software. What he says is entertainment, not advice of any kind.
  • Rent in SOL is an optional tip for a virtual frog. It buys nothing, gives no rewards or returns, is not an investment and cannot be refunded. In-game coins have no value.
  • Greg's trades and bets are paper trades and opinions. He has no keys and cannot move any money.
  • Be kind. No spam, links, hate or personal attacks. We filter and delete notes that break this.
  • Third party names and card images belong to their owners and are shown for commentary.
  • We can change, pause or stop Greg at any time.
